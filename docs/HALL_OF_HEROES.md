# Hall of Assimilation 🏛️

> **Inspired by the pioneers. Honoring the legends.**
>
> *Fewer ZIPs. More credits.*

The [ARS live Hero](../README.md) shows an ever-changing, centered tribute to the independent Farming Simulator modders whose ideas shaped our research. It is deliberately self-aware: **we did not invent everything**. Great modders solved important problems long before ARS — and their names belong in our project's story.

## Inspiration

**Inspiration** means an original mod is credited as a gameplay or integration reference. It does *not* mean ARS copied its source code, that development has started on that specific mod, or that the mod is already replaced.

Explore the **[complete, automatically maintained inspiration list](ASSIMILATION_INDEX.md)** for the original mod names, authors, and direct source links. The public Hero shows a centered selection of those names, without original icons or static per-mod graphics.

### A few pioneers worth thanking

- **[Advanced Damage System](https://github.com/id577/FS25_AdvancedDamageSystem)** by **id577** — for bringing complex machine condition, repairs and workshops into the field. *A broken tractor may be the beginning of a great idea.*
- **[Courseplay](https://github.com/Courseplay/Courseplay_FS25)** by the **Courseplay team** — for proving that farming automation deserves more than a straight line. *Some legends have covered more hectares than the rest of us.*
- **[AutoDrive](https://github.com/Stephan-S/FS25_AutoDrive)** by **Stephan-S and contributors** — for building transport-route workflows across entire maps. *Every road has a story. Many end at the grain silo.*
- **[Soil Work Yield Bonus](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=367297)** by **moji** — for making soil preparation matter. *The plough asked for recognition. The harvest agreed.*

These projects are independent, active community contributions. We do not imply their creators have endorsed, collaborated with, or joined ARS.

## Honored

A mod becomes **Honored** only after its corresponding desired functionality has been **independently implemented in ARS, fully checked for functional parity and published**. No automatic badge from closing a GitHub Issue, no guess based on code activity, no imaginary retirement.

The public **[live index](ASSIMILATION_INDEX.md)** carries the current Honored list. When the first entry qualifies, the Hero will move its name from Inspiration to Honored automatically.

Each future Honored record needs a source-specific human acceptance, a commit-pinned acceptance report, and evidence of a published ARS ZIP release. The verification rules are enforced by the [assimilation proof registry](data/assimilation-proofs.json).

## Our promise

ARS is meant to make many separate gameplay systems work together as one cohesive suite. **We acknowledge the original authors, retain their source links and create our own implementations.** Third-party code, assets, and branding are never silently repackaged as ARS work. Original mod teams keep their own software, licences, identities, and communities.

The broader credits remain in **[Community Inspirations & Thanks](COMMUNITY_INSPIRATIONS.md)**. For actual public product-development focus, see the **[live ARS project home](../README.md)** and **[roadmap](ROADMAP.md)**.

- **Future included third-party resources:** Add a concrete attribution entry for every external asset or component that is actually distributed, recording origin, creator, version (where applicable), licence and notice/location.

## Release checklist

Before any public ARS ZIP: verify its actual contents; include required third-party notices and attribution; ensure redistributed source and assets have the necessary rights; supply the corresponding ARS source as required by EUPL-1.2. Do not publish private files or unreleased game resources.
